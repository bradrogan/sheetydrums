The Trammps - "Disco Inferno" (1976)
Drummer: Earl Young.  129 bpm, 4/4, 92 bars.
Source of ground truth: Drumeo transcription by Mathias Hedegaard Nielsen
  (Licensed For Personal Use Only -- the PDF is NOT committed; only the derived
   per-section hit patterns live in compare_the_trammps_disco_inferno.py).

WHY THIS SONG
  Earl Young's groove is the canonical disco open/closed hi-hat pattern. The
  chart genuinely spans the whole hi-hat range section by section, which makes it
  the primary discriminator for tuning the CheukExpander open/closed split:
    - closed verses                       -> hihat_closed only
    - grooves with offbeat opens (&2, &4) -> mostly closed, a few opens
    - all-open bridge + final chorus      -> hihat_open throughout

CONFIDENCE
  Authoritative: the hi-hat open/closed CHARACTER of every section. Each system's
  noteheads were read at high zoom to tell plain-x (closed) from circled-x (open).
  Approximate: kick (encoded four-on-the-floor) and snare (backbeat 2 & 4) -- the
  song's foundation, good enough for DP bar-alignment but not a note-perfect
  transcription. Fills are approximated by the section groove (plus a 2-bar snare
  roll in the intro). Treat kick/snare F1 as indicative only; the hi-hat columns
  are the measurement this song exists for.
