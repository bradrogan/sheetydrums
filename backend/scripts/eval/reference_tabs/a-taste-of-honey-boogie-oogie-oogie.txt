A Taste Of Honey - "Boogie Oogie Oogie" (1978)
Drummer: Donald R. Johnson.  124 bpm, 4/4, ~148 bars.
Source of ground truth: Drumeo transcription by Mathias Hedegaard Nielsen
  (Licensed For Personal Use Only -- the PDF is NOT committed; only the derived
   per-section hit patterns live in compare_a_taste_of_honey_boogie_oogie_oogie.py).

WHY THIS SONG
  The closed-dominant, high-tempo stress case for the CheukExpander hi-hat split.
  ~90% of the song is relentless SIXTEENTH-note closed hats at 124 bpm. 16ths are
  ~0.12 s apart, so the expander's fixed 0.15-0.35 s "sustain" window straddles the
  next 1-2 strokes -- the aliasing that flips a busy closed groove to "open". The
  choruses add a genuine open-hat passage (disco offbeat barks), so open recall is
  tested too. Pairs with Disco Inferno: this is the "does the fix stop over-firing?"
  anchor; Disco Inferno is the "clean mix" discriminator.

CONFIDENCE
  Authoritative: the hi-hat MODE per section (closed 16ths vs the alternating
  open-16th chorus pattern), read at high zoom. Two conservative simplifications:
    - closed sections encoded as fully-closed 16ths; the occasional single
      phrase-end open "bark" in some intro/verse bars is omitted (treated as
      closed -> at worst a few open false-negatives, never a false open).
    - kick = four-on-the-floor, snare = backbeat 2 & 4 (foundation only, enough
      for DP bar-alignment, not a note-perfect kick/snare transcription).
  Fills approximated by the section groove.
